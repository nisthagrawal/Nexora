# Prototype limitations

- Risk thresholds are prototype configuration, not operational calibration.
- RGB images cannot verify UV ink, chip cryptography, or all holographic features.
- OCR and face matching are probabilistic; unavailable adapters must not be interpreted as a pass.
- Country-specific standards require authorised rule configurations.
- Government/watchlist verification requires an authorised integration and is not present.
- Research datasets and model weights are not included or redistributed.
- Human review is required for uncertain, conflicting, low-quality, or high-risk cases.
